# Luma CMS Vision

## Mission

Luma CMS exists to provide a clean, modular, AI-native alternative to legacy CMS platforms.

It is built for:

- **Developers** — typed APIs, clear module boundaries, predictable architecture
- **Editors** — structured content workflows and visual editing (planned)
- **Agencies** — extensibility without sacrificing security
- **Content teams** — collections, fields, and entries instead of unstructured pages
- **Open-source contributors** — small core, strong contracts, safe extension points

## What we focus on

- Structured content over HTML blobs
- Visual editing (planned, after Content Core)
- Clean, modular architecture
- Secure extensibility through capabilities and extension points
- Developer-first REST APIs with versioning from day one
- Performance and accessibility
- AI-assisted workflows (planned via AI Gateway)

## What we are not

Luma CMS is **not** trying to copy WordPress.

The goal is **not** plugin chaos — unlimited hooks, global state, and unrestricted database access.

The goal **is** a stable content platform with:

- a small core
- official functionality as modules
- plugins as guests with declared capabilities
- named extension points instead of chaotic hooks

## AI-native direction

Luma CMS is designed to support AI-assisted content workflows in the future through a dedicated AI Gateway with strict permissions and audit logging.

AI features are **planned**, not yet implemented. AI output will never be blindly executed as code, SQL, or configuration.
